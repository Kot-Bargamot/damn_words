# Articles: a/an, the, zero article

## Условия употребления

- Use a/an (= one) with singular countable nouns when it's not clear which one. [1]
- Use the (= this, these, that, those) when it is clear which one(s). [1]

## Конструкции

- First‑time mentioning: Sean has a house … and an apartment … [1]
- Talking about somebody’s job: Tina is a programmer. [1]
- Meaning one of several things: Give me an apple, please. [1]
- In expressions of frequency: I go swimming three times a week. [1]
- Exclamations with “What…!”: What a sunny day! [1]
- Description of somebody or something: He is a good person. Tokyo is a beautiful city. [1]
- Second mention with the: … but he prefers to live in the house. [1]
- Listener knows which one: This is the pub I told you about. [1]
- Particular or unique thing/person: Look at the sun. [1]
- Buildings, places, organisations you often see/visit: I go to the gym three times a week. [1]
- Zero article – general plural nouns: Vegetables are good for you. [1]
- Zero article – with home, work, school after at/to/from: I get back from work at 6:20 p.m. [1]
- Zero article – before next/last/this + day/week/etc.: See you next Saturday. [1]
- Zero article – with meals, illnesses, ways of travelling, months, days, periods of time, school subjects: Let’s have dinner … I go to work by bus. [1]
- Zero article – after my, his, her, their, etc.: You can take my car. [1]
