# -ed and -ing adjectives

## Объяснение

- -ed adjectives describe a feeling or state experienced by a person; -ing adjectives describe the thing or situation that causes that feeling. [1]

## Условия употребления

- Use the -ed form when you talk about an emotion or condition (ask “How do I feel?”). Use the -ing form when you talk about the cause of that emotion. [1]

## Конструкции

- ‑ed adjective → subject + be + adjective (e.g., I am bored, She is interested). ‑ing adjective → subject + be + adjective (e.g., The lesson is boring, The topic is interesting). The phrase *interested in* is fixed: interested in + noun or gerund. [1]

## Примеры из материалов

- The book is interesting. — Книга интересная (causes interest). I am interested in the book. — Я заинтересован в книге (feel interest). I am bored. — Мне скучно. The lesson is boring. — Урок скучный. I am interested in music. — Я интересуюсь музыкой. I am interested in learning English. — Я заинтересован в изучении английского. [1]

## Типичные ошибки

- Do not form adjectives by simply adding -ing to any verb; some adjectives have irregular pairs: stressed → stressful, scared → scary, impressed → impressive. [1]
