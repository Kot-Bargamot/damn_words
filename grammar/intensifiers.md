# Intensifiers

## Объяснение

- Intensifiers: very, really, extremely, quite, pretty [1]

## Условия употребления

- Use very, really, extremely to make another word stronger. [1]
- Use quite and pretty (more informal) to make adjectives or adverbs weaker (= a little, but not very). [1]

## Конструкции

- pretty, quite, really, very, extremely + adjective (adverb) [1]
- quite + an article a (if it's a singular countable noun) + adjective + noun [1]

## Примеры из материалов

- This rule is really/ very/ extremely easy. I understood it quickly. [1]
- This park is pretty/ quite crowded, but it's a nice place to walk. [1]
- You're quite a funny guy. [1]
- This is quite a cheap watch. [1]
