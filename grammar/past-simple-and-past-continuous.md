# Past Simple and Past Continuous

## Объяснение

- Past Simple – used to talk about an event, a habit or a state at a definite time in the past. [1]
- Past Continuous – used to talk about events that were in progress at a specific time in the past; also to show that two actions were happening at the same time. [1]

## Условия употребления

- Past Simple is used with yesterday, last week/month/year, three days ago, etc. [1]
- When Past Simple and Past Continuous combine: Past Simple + Past Continuous – e.g., When he came in, she was cooking. [1]
- Past Continuous + when/while Past Continuous – e.g., He was cooking dinner while she was reading. [1]

## Конструкции

- S + V-ed / V2 (affirmative). [1]
- S + did not + V (negative). [1]
- Did + S + V? (question). [1]
- S + was/were + V-ing (affirmative). [1]
- S + was/were + not + V-ing (negative). [1]
- Was/Were + S + V-ing? (question). [1]

## Примеры из материалов

- I started my new job two days ago. [1]
- She didn't come back yesterday. [1]
- Did you finish that task yesterday? [1]
- I was working on my new project yesterday at 10 o'clock. [1]
- She wasn't listening to music at that moment. [1]
- Were you studying at 2 p.m.? [1]
